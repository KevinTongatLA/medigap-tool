📊 Context & Key Financial Assumptions

To help you evaluate these options with confidence, this assessment projects cumulative lifetime costs across a 25-year horizon (Age 65 to 90) using the following baseline parameters:

  • Healthcare Rate Increase: 5.0% compounded annually (combining medical inflation + age-attained tier increases).
  • Medicare Part B Base Premium: Indexed from $202.90/month base with annual CPI adjustments.
  • Medicare Part B Deductible: Starts at $283/year and grows with medical inflation.
  • High-Deductible Plan G Threshold: Based on the $2,950 calendar-year deductible cap.
  • Plan G Advantage: Guarantees $0 Out-of-Pocket Usage Expenses after the Part B deductible is met.
